# The Workbench summarises a Session's sections and the Inspector shows them in full

The Workbench gives an overview of a Session's Changes, Agents and Background Tasks; the Inspector shows each section in full. Earlier designs called the full surface Stage. On 2026-10-21, the owner chose to retain Inspector and update the docs and Paper to match the existing code. The Workbench remains a separate surface and keeps the Plugin card Slot recorded in [ADR-0035](0035-plugins-add-to-named-slots-and-never-edit-core-screens.md).

## Decision

- **The Workbench holds desktop and web cards over a Session's page, in its right gutter,** toggled from the Session header. The owner amended placement on 2026-10-29 (#1344): the Feed and Composer share one centered axis under the 800 cap. When both side gutters fit a 312-wide card and its gaps, the cards occupy the right gutter without moving that axis. Otherwise they auto-hide; an explicit Show floats them absolutely over the content's trailing edge (owner, 2026-11-02), never as a column that squeezes the Feed. The mounted draft and the person's visibility choice survive resizing. The owner amended placement again on 2026-11-10: the Workbench is drawn absolutely over the page and never takes width from it, so there is no separate docked region; only the list and the Inspector narrow the page, and the Feed's scrollbar stays at the page's trailing edge. The owner amended placement a third time on 2026-11-10, replacing the both-gutters rule and the fixed axis above: the cards show whenever the page's two gutters together hold a card and its gaps, not only when each does. Where the trailing gutter alone is too narrow, the Feed and Composer keep their width and move aside together, off the page's centre, so the cards never cover them; they return to the centre when the cards hide. The overlay below that budget has no backing, so the Feed shows through the cards' glass. There is one Workbench per Session; it is not a second sidebar, and the rail is still where sections live.
- **Nothing the Workbench owns is drawn in a header.** Pull requests, checks, Changes counts and file changes are the Workbench's, on every platform. A Session header carries identity and state only: title, status, the running Turn, the Workbench toggle and "…". A header is a fixed width and a Session's Changes are an unbounded list, so the two never fit.
- **The Workbench holds section cards, and it is an overview of what is happening in the Session.** Argo's cards are Changes, Agents and Background Tasks; the Agents card counts the Agents the Session started, not the Installed Agents the Agents page lists. Amended by the owner on 2026-11-10: a Started Session is not one of those Agents; it is listed under its starter in the Sessions list and never drawn in the Workbench or Inspector. A card is drawn only when the Session has something for it, so a Session with no Agents has no Agents card, as its header has no Agents button.
- **Cards summarize their sections.** The Changes card shows a diff total and a bounded preview; the Agents and Background Tasks cards draw only their running items, up to three rows, and end in an "N done" footer that opens the full list (owner, 2026-11-02). Cards use kit surfaces. Their Inspector lists use `List` and `ListItem` ([ADR-0032](0032-one-list-and-one-list-item-drawn-by-argo-everywhere.md)).
- **The Changes card previews up to three Open Changes.** The owner approved [WorkbenchGlass / Changes](https://app.paper.design/file/01M4RJWCAC00EA21V8BKY91FEV/p-4-0/218W-0) on 2026-10-21, replacing this card's summary-only rule. Rows show a PR number, title, diff totals when supplied, individual checks and an actions menu. A grouped remainder counts additional Open Changes and other stages, with overlapping stage marks. The full header toggles the preview. Change rows and the grouped remainder open Changes in the Inspector.
- **The Agents card also previews active Agents.** The owner approved [WorkbenchGlass / Agents](https://app.paper.design/file/01M4RJWCAC00EA21V8BKY91FEV/p-4-0/21BX-0) on 2026-10-21, replacing this card's summary-only rule. Active rows show role avatars, status marks and Agent details. On 2026-11-02 the owner replaced the grouped count of completed Agents: the card draws only running Agents, up to three, and an "N done" footer (counting the rest) opens the full list. A row opens that Agent's Feed in the Inspector. The full header toggles the preview. Pressing the body opens the full Agents list in the Inspector.
- **A Change is what the Changes card counts and its Inspector tab lists.** One line of work, named by its branch, at one of five stages: Working, Branch, Open, Merged or Closed. A Session can have several Changes at once, each at its own stage, so no design may assume one pull request.
- **Plugins add their own cards through a named Slot,** under [ADR-0035](0035-plugins-add-to-named-slots-and-never-edit-core-screens.md) unchanged: the Plugin draws inside its card, Argo decides where the card goes, in what order and how much fits, and a Plugin never moves, hides or restyles Argo's cards or another Plugin's. This Slot is the one ADR-0035 declares for a Plugin page beside the Feed, now under the Workbench's name. A Plugin's `header` action may open its card, as ADR-0035 says. Like Argo's own cards, a Plugin's card is drawn only when it has something to show (Spec 0026, #1864): a card page whose Render returns `null` answers the empty root, and the card, its Inspector tab, its phone row and a header action that targets it are all absent until the page answers with a tree.
- **Pressing a Workbench card's body or an item opens its full view in the Inspector, as an Inspector tab.** The owner clarified on 2026-10-21 that the whole collapsible header toggles its preview. The Workbench stays where the width budget permits. The Inspector is a surface, not a section: it is always reached from a card, never from the rail.
- **An Inspector tab is a full view, and it holds a list.** The Agents tab lists the Agents the Session started, grouped and counted by state; the Changes and Background Tasks tabs list theirs the same way. Pressing a row opens that item's detail in the same tab — a Change's diff, an Agent's Feed, a Background Task's output — and Back returns to the list. An Inspector tab therefore has at least two levels, and a Plugin's tab is whatever the Plugin draws.
- **Phones reach the same content, and the design says how.** Every Workbench card, Plugin cards included, and every Inspector tab is reachable on compact widths, where there is no column. The presentation is being settled in Paper and is not fixed here; what is fixed is that nothing is dropped on phones and no card is desktop-only.
- **Each card and each Inspector tab get a Paper design before code,** as every new visible state does and as ADR-0035 requires of each Slot.

## Considered options

- **Keep pull requests and checks in the Session header,** as the meta line and a Merge button did until 2026-10-20. Rejected by the owner: a header cannot hold several Changes, and a "+N" dropdown over an unbounded list is the variable-width problem again.
- **Give Changes its own rail section.** Rejected: there is one sidebar and the rail is for Plugin sections (owner, 2026-10-19). Changes belong to a Session, so they belong beside its Feed.
- **Let Plugins place their cards themselves, or reorder Argo's.** Rejected by ADR-0035, for the reasons it gives.
- **Let a Workbench card hold its section's list, and give the Inspector one item.** Rejected by the owner: the Workbench is an overview of what is happening in the Session, and 312 of column cannot carry three unbounded lists. A bounded preview in Changes and the approved Agents preview are exceptions. The full section and its detail still belong in the Inspector.
- **Keep three persistent columns at every width.** Rejected: at 312 plus 800 plus an Inspector the Feed loses its width. The 2026-10-29 placement amendment budgets the Inspector and Workbench around protected content instead. Only an explicitly expanded Inspector replaces the primary view; the Workbench overlays it, transiently, only on an explicit Show below the budget; no persistent panel covers its Feed or Composer.
- **Settle the phone presentation here,** as a sheet or as a drill-in. Left to the design: the compact layout for the cards and the Inspector is open in Paper, and an ADR guessing at it would be a decision nobody made.
