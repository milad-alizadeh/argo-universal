# The Workbench summarises a Session's sections and the Inspector shows them in full

The Workbench gives an overview of a Session's Changes, Agents and Shells; the Inspector shows each section in full. Earlier designs called the full surface Stage. On 2026-10-21, the owner chose to retain Inspector and update the docs and Paper to match the existing code. The Workbench remains a separate surface and keeps the Plugin card Slot recorded in [ADR-0035](0035-plugins-add-to-named-slots-and-never-edit-core-screens.md).

## Decision

- **The Workbench holds desktop and web cards beside a Session's Feed,** toggled from the Session header. The owner amended placement on 2026-10-29 (#1344): the Feed and Composer share one centered axis under the 800 cap. When both side gutters fit a 312-wide card and its gaps, the cards occupy the right gutter without moving that axis. Otherwise they take a separate region only while the content keeps its protected width. Below that budget they auto-hide; an explicit Show opens a dedicated Workbench view instead of covering the Feed or Composer. The mounted draft and the person's visibility choice survive resizing. There is one Workbench per Session; it is not a second sidebar, and the rail is still where sections live.
- **Nothing the Workbench owns is drawn in a header.** Pull requests, checks, Changes counts and file changes are the Workbench's, on every platform. A Session header carries identity and state only: title, status, the running Turn, the Workbench toggle and "…". A header is a fixed width and a Session's Changes are an unbounded list, so the two never fit.
- **The Workbench holds section cards, and it is an overview of what is happening in the Session.** Argo's cards are Changes, Agents and Shells; the Agents card counts the Agents the Session started, not the Installed Agents the Agents page lists. A card is drawn only when the Session has something for it, so a Session with no Agents has no Agents card, as its header has no Agents button.
- **Cards summarize their sections.** The Changes card shows a diff total and a bounded preview; the Shells card shows a count. Cards use kit surfaces. Their Inspector lists use `List` and `ListItem` ([ADR-0032](0032-one-list-and-one-list-item-drawn-by-argo-everywhere.md)).
- **The Changes card previews up to three Open Changes.** The owner approved [WorkbenchGlass / Changes](https://app.paper.design/file/01M4RJWCAC00EA21V8BKY91FEV/p-4-0/218W-0) on 2026-10-21, replacing this card's summary-only rule. Rows show a PR number, title, diff totals when supplied, individual checks and an actions menu. A grouped remainder counts additional Open Changes and other stages, with overlapping stage marks. The full header toggles the preview. Change rows and the grouped remainder open Changes in the Inspector.
- **The Agents card also previews active Agents.** The owner approved [WorkbenchGlass / Agents](https://app.paper.design/file/01M4RJWCAC00EA21V8BKY91FEV/p-4-0/21BX-0) on 2026-10-21, replacing this card's summary-only rule. Active rows show role avatars, status marks and Agent details. Completed Agents share one count with up to three overlapping role avatars. The full header toggles the preview. Pressing the body opens the full Agents list in the Inspector.
- **A Change is what the Changes card counts and its Inspector tab lists.** One line of work, named by its branch, at one of five stages: Working, Branch, Open, Merged or Closed. A Session can have several Changes at once, each at its own stage, so no design may assume one pull request.
- **Plugins add their own cards through a named Slot,** under [ADR-0035](0035-plugins-add-to-named-slots-and-never-edit-core-screens.md) unchanged: the Plugin draws inside its card, Argo decides where the card goes, in what order and how much fits, and a Plugin never moves, hides or restyles Argo's cards or another Plugin's. This Slot is the one ADR-0035 declares for a Plugin page beside the Feed, now under the Workbench's name. A Plugin's `header` action may open its card, as ADR-0035 says.
- **Pressing a Workbench card's body or an item opens its full view in the Inspector, as an Inspector tab.** The owner clarified on 2026-10-21 that the whole collapsible header toggles its preview. The Workbench stays where the width budget permits. The Inspector is a surface, not a section: it is always reached from a card, never from the rail.
- **An Inspector tab is a full view, and it holds a list.** The Agents tab lists the Agents the Session started, grouped and counted by state; the Changes and Shells tabs list theirs the same way. Pressing a row opens that item's detail in the same tab — a Change's diff, an Agent's Feed, a Shell's output — and Back returns to the list. An Inspector tab therefore has at least two levels, and a Plugin's tab is whatever the Plugin draws.
- **Phones reach the same content, and the design says how.** Every Workbench card, Plugin cards included, and every Inspector tab is reachable on compact widths, where there is no column. The presentation is being settled in Paper and is not fixed here; what is fixed is that nothing is dropped on phones and no card is desktop-only.
- **Each card and each Inspector tab get a Paper design before code,** as every new visible state does and as ADR-0035 requires of each Slot.

## Considered options

- **Keep pull requests and checks in the Session header,** as the meta line and a Merge button did until 2026-10-20. Rejected by the owner: a header cannot hold several Changes, and a "+N" dropdown over an unbounded list is the variable-width problem again.
- **Give Changes its own rail section.** Rejected: there is one sidebar and the rail is for Plugin sections (owner, 2026-10-19). Changes belong to a Session, so they belong beside its Feed.
- **Let Plugins place their cards themselves, or reorder Argo's.** Rejected by ADR-0035, for the reasons it gives.
- **Let a Workbench card hold its section's list, and give the Inspector one item.** Rejected by the owner: the Workbench is an overview of what is happening in the Session, and 312 of column cannot carry three unbounded lists. A bounded preview in Changes and the approved Agents preview are exceptions. The full section and its detail still belong in the Inspector.
- **Keep three persistent columns at every width.** Rejected: at 312 plus 800 plus an Inspector the Feed loses its width. The 2026-10-29 placement amendment budgets the Inspector and Workbench around protected content instead. Only an explicitly expanded Inspector or dedicated Workbench view replaces the primary view; persistent panels never cover its Feed or Composer.
- **Settle the phone presentation here,** as a sheet or as a drill-in. Left to the design: the compact layout for the cards and the Inspector is open in Paper, and an ADR guessing at it would be a decision nobody made.
