# A Screen header offers a title menu and page options

The Jira Plugin's project page had a full-width List/Board `SegmentedControl` above its issues, a Filter menu, a view menu and an overflow menu holding "Change Jira project" and "Change Jira site". On a 390 pt phone that is four icons beside the title. The owner chose two options from the Paper exploration "Jira Explorations · Project header on phone" (#2323) and combined them (owner, 2026-11-18). From option E, the title picks the project. From option C, one Options button holds the view, the filters and the sort. A phone header then shows only Options and Search. The Screen header had neither piece, and any page that shows one of several scopes, or has view and filter choices, needs them.

## Decision

- **`Screen.titleMenu`** takes Actions and `Action.Section`s, as `actions` does ([ADR-0043](0043-a-screen-declares-its-header-once.md)). The title then draws a down chevron and opens these Actions as the header's own menu: Radix on the web, a SwiftUI Menu on iOS and a Compose DropdownMenu on Android. This is where iOS and Android put a scope picker. On the web the title stays the page's heading, and the heading holds a WAI-ARIA menu button named by the title, which Tab reaches and Enter, Space or Arrow Down opens. On iOS and Android the accessible element is the system menu's button, named by the title: VoiceOver and TalkBack read a button with its menu, not a heading, because one native element cannot carry both roles. The menu opens below the title, lined up with its start. On a phone's native stack header, the menu button takes the title's place, and a later draw without a menu gives the bar its own title back. There the title is inline, because UIKit's large title is the system's own text and cannot open a menu.
- **`Screen.options`** holds the page's options: a `SegmentedControl`, a `FieldGroup`, a `Stack`, a `Button` or `Text`. **`Screen.optionsBadge`** is a count or a status dot on their button. The header draws one Options button (the `filters` icon) before its other Actions. On a phone the button opens the options as a sheet. On a wide window it is a labelled pill ("Options · 2") that opens them as a popover below it. The App owns whether the options are open. A Plugin's choices inside them change its Page state at once, and the sheet stays open across the redraws they cause. When the Screen draws its own heading, the sheet hangs on the heading's button, so a wide window anchors the popover there. A navigator's header button, and the band's on a wide window for a header-only Screen, only opens it, and the Screen holds the sheet inside its own tree. The band draws the title's menu too. Options that go away close, so they never come back open. The kit `Sheet` gains a kit-only `popoverPlacement` for this, because the Composer's popovers open above their pills.
- **A sheet insets its content once.** A sheet that pads composed content, such as page options or a Plugin's sheet, marks it as the `inset` list place. There a `FieldGroup` draws as it does in a Screen's aside: it is sized to its rows, draws no inset of its own, and on the web its section titles start on the cards' edge. The view switch, the section headings and the cards then share one edge, as an iOS sheet's do (owner, 2026-11-18). A phone's Popover, which is a Sheet too, keeps a page's place for its rows.
- **A page's view switch lives in its options** as a `SegmentedControl`. A header never shows a full-width segmented bar (the amendment to [ADR-0042](0042-field-groups-and-lists-are-separate-kit-components.md)).
- **Both slots are additive.** By [ADR-0034](0034-plugin-apps-check-one-wire-version.md), the Plugin wire version and its minimum stay at 9. An older App ignores the new slots and draws the title as text.

## Considered options

- **A header submenu per choice** (#2323's first version: a Filter menu and a "Show issues as" menu). Rejected: two icons on a phone, and the ⋮ stayed for the project and site.
- **A full-width segmented bar under the header** (Paper option D). Rejected: it costs 48 pt of height on every phone page, and it is the bar the owner called out.
- **Let a header `Action` open a Sheet the Plugin keeps open in Page state.** Rejected: every page would carry the open flag in its address, and on a wide window the sheet had no button to anchor a popover on.
